Module 06

Discovery
Questions

He who asks the questions controls the conversation.

Business & Leads

  • 1. How do you currently get most of your clients?
  • 2. How much are you spending monthly on portals (MagicBricks/Practo/JustDial)?
  • 3. What is your average deal size or patient ticket size?
  • 4. What is your biggest frustration with your current lead quality?
  • 5. Who do you consider your biggest local competitor?
  • 6. What percentage of your business comes from referrals?
  • 7. What is your peak season for business?
  • 8. How many leads do you need per month to hit your growth goals?
  • 9. Are you losing deals because clients don't trust you quickly enough?
  • 10. Where do you want your business to be 12 months from now?

Digital Presence

  • 11. Do you currently have a website?
  • 12. Does your website actually generate inquiries for you?
  • 13. Have you claimed and verified your Google Business Profile?
  • 14. Are you actively collecting Google reviews from clients?
  • 15. Have you ever hired an agency or freelancer before?
  • 16. If yes, why did you stop working with them?
  • 17. Have you ever searched for your own services on Google to see who shows up?
  • 18. Are you using WhatsApp Business for client communication?
  • 19. Can your current team handle 20-30 more inquiries a month?
  • 20. Is anyone in your team responsible for digital updates right now?

Module 08

Objection
Handling

12 real conversations. Don't freeze, follow the script.

1. "Not Interested"

"Nahi, abhi interest nahi hai."
"Kya 'not interested' kyunki digital kaam nahi karega, ya pehle kisi ne dhokha diya?"
"Pehle try kiya, kuch nahi hua."
"Toh problem digital nahi tha, execution tha. Bas 5 minutes dijye, main dikhata hoon kyun fail hua."

2. "Too Expensive"

"Bahut mehenga hai."
"Kis comparison mein? MagicBricks ₹20K monthly leta hai. Website ₹18,999 one-time hai. 3 saal chalti hai. Long term mein ye portal se sasta aur profitable hai."

3. "Already Have Website"

"Website already bani hui hai."
"Sir website kaam karne se fayda deti hai, sirf hone se nahi. Kya direct leads aa rahe hain us se?"
"Nahi, bas information ke liye banayi thi."
"Toh woh online brochure hai. Main usey lead-generation machine banana chahunga."

4. "Already Have Developer"

"Mera developer already hai."
"Great. Last update mein kitna time laga tha? Kya woh SEO aur GBP bhi handle karte hain?"
"Nahi bas design karte hain."
"Toh gap yahin hai. Car hai, driver nahi. Main driver, GPS, aur fuel teeno dunga."

5. "Send WhatsApp"

"Aap details WhatsApp kar do."
"Zaroor bhej dunga. Lekin sirf details padh ke decision nahi banta. Main audit ka screenshot bhej raha hoon. Aap dekhiye, phir hum detail call karte hain."

6. "Call Later"

"Abhi busy hoon, baad mein call karna."
"No problem. 'Baad mein' kab suit karega? Tuesday 11 AM ya Thursday 4 PM? Bas 10 minutes chahiye hongi."

7. "Need Approval"

"Partner se pooch ke batata hoon."
"Jab unhe batayenge, woh puchenge 'kitne ka hai?' aur value miss ho jayegi. Better hai main aap dono ke saath 15-min zoom call kar loon."

8. "Need Discount"

"Kuch discount kar do yaar."
"Main cheap vendor nahi hoon sir. Lekin ek option hai — ya toh ek service hata dein package se, ya payment terms 30-30-40 kar dun?"

9. "Using Portals"

"MagicBricks se kaam chal raha hai acha khasa."
"Woh aapke landlord hain, client nahi. Kal rent badha denge ya same lead 5 logo ko denge. Main aapka apna digital ghar banana sikha raha hoon."

10. "Referrals Are Enough"

"Hamara sara kaam referrals se aata hai."
"Referrals gold hain lekin unpredictable hain. Digital ek predictable pipeline banata hai. Plus, referral aane ke baad bhi log Google pe check karte hain trust ke liye."

Daily SOP

Consistency over intensity.

Morning Routine (9-10 AM)

  • • Check CRM & pipeline
  • • Reply to WhatsApp messages
  • • Prep today's call list
  • • Research top 5 prospects
  • • Set daily targets
MONEY TIME

Core Hours (10 AM-5 PM)

  • • 25-30 Dials minimum
  • • 3-5 Meaningful conversations
  • • 15-20 WhatsApp follow-ups
  • • Live CRM updates
  • • Send proposals within 2 hours

Evening (5-6 PM)

  • • Log all calls & notes
  • • Schedule tomorrow's tasks
  • • Send daily report to founder
  • • Review 1 objection faced
  • • Practice 1 script aloud

NEVER DO LIST

  • ✗ Call without prior Google research.
  • ✗ Promise technical features we don't build.
  • ✗ Give discounts without founder approval.
  • ✗ Bad-mouth their current portal or competitors.
  • ✗ Skip CRM data entry.
  • ✗ Ghost a "maybe" prospect. Follow up until Yes/No.

Role Play
Conversations

Read these out loud with a partner daily.

Role Play 1: Real Estate Broker (Cold to Meeting)

"Hello Amit sir, Harshdeep here. Main dekh raha tha aap MagicBricks pe premium properties list karte hain Gurgaon mein, right?"
"Haan boliye."
"Sir honestly ek observation thi. Jab koi buyer 'DLF Camellias broker' Google karta hai, aapka naam nahi aata. Woh traffic seedha aapke competitors ke paas ja raha hai jo SEO use kar rahe hain. Kya hum 15 minute is gap pe baat kar sakte hain?"
"Abhi toh time nahi hai."
"Main samajhta hoon sir. Agar main Thursday 4 PM ek short zoom invite bhejun, just to show you the data without any pitch, chalega?"

Role Play 2: Doctor/Clinic (Handling "Not Interested")

"Website wagera mein interest nahi hai mujhe."
"Doctor, I completely respect that. Lekin kya aap nayi patient inquiries badhane mein interested hain?"
"Patients toh sabko chahiye, par yeh website ka chakkar time consuming hota hai."
"Exactly why humari delivery 7 days mein hoti hai without taking your time. Ek aur clinic yahan Noida mein humare GBP optimization se daily 3 appointment calls extra generate kar rahi hai. Kal 12 baje mil sakte hain to show you how?"
"Theek hai, kal 12 baje aana."

Role Play 3: CA/Lawyer (Handling "Referrals Only")

"Humara CA firm 15 saal se chal raha hai. Sara kaam network aur referrals se aata hai."
"Referrals gold hain sir. Lekin kya aapko pata hai aaj kal log referral milne ke baad bhi call karne se pehle Google pe background check karte hain?"
"Haan karte toh honge."
"Wahi problem hai. Jab woh search karte hain, unhe ek 2-star rating ya outdated page milta hai. Jo client convert hona tha, woh doubt mein chala jata hai. Main aapki digital credibility match karna chahta hoon aapke 15 saal ke experience se. 10 minute lenge meeting mein?"

Role Play 4: "Too Expensive" Deep Dive to Close

"Growth pack ka ₹18,999 bahut expensive lag raha hai yaar. Freelancers toh 5000 mein bana dete hain."
"Bikul sir, 5000 mein website ban jayegi. Lekin woh sirf dikhne wali hogi, bikne wali nahi. Hum yahan business asset bana rahe hain. Agar yeh setup mahine ki sirf 1 extra lead bhi laaye, toh aapka ROI kitna hoga?"
"Lead aayegi tab na."
"Understood. Agar price abhi barrier hai, toh main suggest karunga hum Launch Package (₹12,999) se start karte hain. Base foundation ban jayegi, later hum GBP aur Growth pe upgrade kar lenge. Deal?"
"Thik hai, terms bhej do uske."
